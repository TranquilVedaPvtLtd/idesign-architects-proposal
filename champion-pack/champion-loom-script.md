# Champion Loom Script - iDesign Architects

**Purpose:** A 90-second Loom that walks Swati through the proposal so she can forward one link to her CA, her managers, or anyone on her side. Shruti records this on the day the proposal is sent.

**Recording setup:**
- Tool: Loom (browser extension) or Zoom record + trim
- Screen: proposal open at the live URL
- Camera: small bubble in bottom-right, face on
- Background: plain wall, good light
- Record in one take if possible; if a retake is needed, keep under 2 minutes total
- Use a wired or bluetooth earphone mic, not laptop mic

## Shruti's notes to self before recording

- Open the Loom on the *hero* of the proposal - the Proposal Engine workflow block is the money shot; do not rush past it.
- Say Swati's name at least twice (opening + close). It raises reply rate.
- Do NOT say "I hope this finds you well", "please let me know", or "looking forward to hearing from you" - they are tells that this is a template.
- If you fumble a price, keep going - never stop mid-Loom. Loom viewers forgive small stumbles, they do not forgive awkward restarts.
- When the Loom is done, share: `https://www.loom.com/share/<id>` in the WhatsApp message along with the proposal link.
