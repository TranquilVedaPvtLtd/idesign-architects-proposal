# Champion Loom Script - iDesign Architects

**Purpose:** A 90-second Loom that walks Swati through the proposal so she can forward one link to her CA, her managers, or anyone on her side. Shruti records this on the day the proposal is sent.

**Recording setup:**
- Tool: Loom (browser extension) or Zoom record + trim
- Screen: proposal open at [local URL or cinematic site URL when live]
- Camera: small bubble in bottom-right, face on
- Background: plain wall, good light
- Record in one take if possible; if a retake is needed, keep under 2 minutes total
- Use a wired or bluetooth earphone mic, not laptop mic

---

## The script (90 seconds, timed)

**[0:00 - 0:15] The opening (webcam full frame, no screen share yet)**

> "Hi Swati This is Shruti from TranquilVeda. Thank you for the call last week - I heard everything you said about the seven Excel sheets, the 2pm and 4pm standups, and how revisions are getting lost between the BOQ and the cashflow. I've put together a proposal that is built specifically for your studio, not a template. Let me walk you through it."

*[switch to screen share, scroll to hero]*

---

**[0:15 - 0:40] Scroll through hero + context (30s, talk over the scroll)**

> "This is what I'm proposing - a Studio Command Centre. One system for every project, every rupee, every drawing. We stay out of your SketchUp and AutoCAD entirely. The top of the page shows the price - five lakh fifty thousand one-time, plus fifteen thousand a month. We'll come back to price.
>
> Right here *[pause on context block]*, I've written what I heard from you - seven to eight sheets per client, around thirty live projects, a BOQ taking twenty to thirty hours, and a team of four to eight designers plus two managers reporting verbally. If any of this is off, tell me and I'll rewrite."

---

**[0:40 - 1:05] Walk through pricing + what we have built (25s)**

> "Three ways to start, and the middle one is the recommended one. On the left, a sixty-thousand rupee Pilot - we migrate one live project in three weeks, you see the system work, and if you decide to go ahead the sixty thousand comes off the full price. In the middle, the full Command Centre at five lakh fifty thousand setup plus fifteen thousand monthly. On the right, if you don't like recurring fees, a prepaid version that bundles the first year into one payment.
>
> Right below that *[scroll down]*, four similar builds we've shipped - a real estate portal with an AI chatbot, a WhatsApp AI for a mutual fund distributor, a sports retailer's full digital turnaround, and a financial advisor's WhatsApp agent. Client names are under NDA but we can arrange reference calls."

---

**[1:05 - 1:25] Risk handling + final CTA (20s)**

> "Before the close, I've put in the five things that can go wrong and what we do about each. The most important one is the week-six adoption checkpoint - if your team isn't using the system by week six, we pause and refund thirty percent. It's in the SOW, not just a promise.
>
> If this looks right, WhatsApp me on nine six seven three seven five eight seven seven seven. If you want to show this to your CA or anyone on your team, there's a stakeholder grid at the bottom that tells them where to jump in. I'm also sending you a three-slide deck you can share with anyone internally."

---

**[1:25 - 1:30] Sign-off (5s, back to webcam)**

> "Thank you, Swati Reply any time. I'll be online today."

## Shruti's notes to self before recording

- Look at the camera, not the screen, during the opening and sign-off.
- Say the prospect's name at least twice (opening + close). It raises reply rate.
- Do NOT say "I hope this finds you well", "please let me know", or "looking forward to hearing from you" - they are tells that this is a template.
- If you fumble the price, keep going - never stop mid-Loom. Loom viewers forgive small stumbles, they do not forgive awkward restarts.
- When the Loom is done, share with: `https://www.loom.com/share/<id>` in the WhatsApp message along with the proposal link.
