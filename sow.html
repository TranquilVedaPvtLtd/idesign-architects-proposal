Section 1

Project Overview

Jarvis Daily will deploy a private, production-ready instance of the Proposal Engine for iDesign Architects (Pune), configured around iDesign's own templates, rate library, project schema and brand. Through this engagement iDesign will:

  • Generate AI-drafted client proposals in iDesign's firm voice, typically within 90 seconds
  • Edit any drafted proposal in natural language (English, Marathi or Hindi), with visible diff and per-edit rollback
  • Send approved proposals as cinematic websites on iDesign's subdomain, with one-click PDF export and WhatsApp-on-approval
  • Retain full ownership and export rights over every proposal, client record, rate revision and piece of business data
  • Maintain an immutable audit trail of every proposal version, edit and send action, retained for 8 years (MCA Rule 3 compliance)

The system is hosted on managed infrastructure procured and maintained by Jarvis Daily. Build duration is 4 calendar weeks from kickoff, followed by 21 calendar days of hypercare with daily check-ins, then a 12-month retainer.

Section 10

Important Notes

System Design

The Proposal Engine is grounded on iDesign Architects' own data: the firm's three standard documents, the rate library, and the 5 historical approved proposals. Generic large language models will not produce comparable results because they lack this private, proprietary context. The monthly retainer covers all model API usage up to 60 drafts per calendar month; drafts beyond that are billed at ₹25 each.

Adoption Checkpoint (Week 6 from go-live)

A formal adoption review takes place six weeks after go-live. If fewer than 40% of iDesign Architects' new client proposals initiated after go-live have been generated through the Engine by this date, both parties shall convene in good faith within 7 business days to assess the adoption barriers (training gaps, workflow friction, data quality issues, role coverage) and to agree any necessary adjustments to onboarding cadence, training inputs, or scope. No automatic refund is triggered by the adoption checkpoint.

Upgrade Path to Studio Command Centre

Should iDesign Architects subsequently elect to expand to the full Studio Command Centre engagement (a separate SOW) within 90 calendar days of final handover, the ₹1,50,000 Proposal Engine setup fee shall be credited 100% against the Studio Command Centre setup fee. The Studio Command Centre adds the BOQ engine, milestone payment tracker, cashflow rollup, contractor payment dashboard, mobile site-visit capture, and team WhatsApp surface.

Cancellation

Either party may terminate this SOW with 30 calendar days written notice. Setup fees already invoiced are non-refundable. Monthly retainer is pro-rated for the termination month. On termination, iDesign Architects' data is exported, retrieved and deleted per the procedure in Section 11.4. Jarvis Daily may terminate for material breach, insolvency, non-payment beyond 30 days past invoice due date, or failure to fulfil the 1 hour per week UAT commitment beyond two consecutive review weeks.

Liability, Confidentiality and Governing Law

Both parties shall treat as confidential any non-public information shared under this engagement, surviving termination for 3 years. Jarvis Daily's total aggregate liability under this SOW is capped at fees paid in the three calendar months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, or for delays caused by force majeure events. This SOW is governed by Indian law; courts at Pune, Maharashtra have exclusive jurisdiction. Disputes will first be referred to good faith negotiation; if unresolved within 30 days, to sole-arbitrator arbitration at Pune under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Digital signatures are valid under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Section 11

Data Ownership and Intellectual Property

11.1 Data Ownership

iDesign Architects owns all data and materials processed through the Proposal Engine, including all proposals generated, the firm's rate library and rate revisions, BOQ inputs and outputs, project schemas, template content authored by iDesign, brand assets, pricing benchmarks, client lists, contact records, project details, and the audit trail of edits and approvals. Jarvis Daily processes this data solely to deliver the services under this SOW. iDesign retains full access and export rights at any time during the engagement and for 90 calendar days after termination.

11.2 Admin Access

At go-live, Jarvis Daily hands over to iDesign's nominated team the credentials for the Proposal Engine admin panel, which provides role-based access to view, search, edit and export all of the data referred to in Section 11.1. Jarvis Daily's internal Platform infrastructure, including AI model API keys, source code repositories, build pipelines, hosting accounts and CI/CD systems, remains with Jarvis Daily as part of the Platform IP described in Section 11.5.

11.3 Purpose of Use

iDesign's data is used for one purpose only: delivering the services under this SOW. It is not sold, not used to train general-purpose AI models, not shared with any other Jarvis Daily client, and not disclosed to outside parties. Data is routed through the third-party providers listed in Section 11.6 only as strictly required to operate the Proposal Engine.

Jarvis Daily may collect and use de-identified, aggregated telemetry from Platform operations (for example, latency metrics, error rates, prompt-success rates, feature-usage patterns) to improve Platform reliability and performance. Such telemetry shall under no circumstances include, and shall not be construed to grant Jarvis Daily any right to use, disclose or commercialise: iDesign's rate library, rate cards or pricing benchmarks; iDesign's project schema, BOQ structure or template content; iDesign's client lists, project pipeline or business intelligence; or any content of iDesign's proposals, in original or derivative form.

11.4 Data Export and Deletion on Termination

On termination of this SOW for any reason, iDesign shall receive a complete export of all data referred to in Section 11.1 in CSV or JSON format, delivered within 21 business days of the termination date, subject to all outstanding invoices being cleared. iDesign retains export and retrieval rights for 90 calendar days following the termination date.

Jarvis Daily then deletes iDesign's data from active production systems. Encrypted rolling backups purge on their natural cycle (up to 60 calendar days). Data retained by third-party providers, or data Jarvis Daily is required to retain for statutory compliance, dispute resolution, or defence of legal claims, is excluded from this deletion. All data handling complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; iDesign is the data fiduciary and Jarvis Daily operates as a data processor on documented instructions.
