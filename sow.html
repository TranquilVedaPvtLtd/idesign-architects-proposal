This Scope of Work ("SOW") sets out the terms under which Tranquil Veda Private Limited ("Service Provider") will design, build, deploy and support the Studio Command Centre ("the Platform") for iDesign Architects ("Client"). This SOW is to be read together with the accompanying proposal (ref. JAR-IDA-001, dated 19 April 2026).

1. Scope and deliverables

1.1 Engagement

The engagement is a fixed-scope, phased build of the Studio Command Centre. The engagement period begins on the date of signature of this SOW and receipt of the first tranche of the setup fee, whichever is later. The estimated build duration is 10 to 12 calendar weeks, followed by 30 calendar days of hypercare.

1.3 Exclusions (out of scope)

  • 3D rendering, moodboard generation, SketchUp, AutoCAD or any CAD/BIM file authoring.
  • HR, payroll or salary processing for Client or Client's team.
  • Client-facing website redesign or public web properties beyond the internal Command Centre.
  • Public website chatbot, available as a separate Phase D engagement, KPI-gated.
  • VLM (vision-language model) drawing-to-BOQ automated extraction, available as a separate Phase D engagement, KPI-gated.
  • Backfill of projects older than the last 20 completed projects as of signature date.
  • Content creation (photography, copywriting, brand collateral) beyond training videos.
  • Third-party software licence fees invoiced directly to Client (see section 3).

2. Engagement commitments from Client

The Client undertakes:

  • To nominate a single point of contact (typically the Principal Architect) authorised to sign off on schema mappings, BOQ logic, proposal drafts and acceptance of deliverables.
  • To commit a minimum of 2 hours per week of the nominated point of contact's time for User Acceptance Testing (UAT) during the full build period. This is a material commitment, reviewed at week 2.
  • To provide timely access to representative historical data (last 20 projects), current Excel workbooks, rate library, past BOQs and past client proposals.
  • To confirm receipt of each deliverable within 7 business days of sharing. Silence beyond 7 business days constitutes deemed acceptance.
  • To allocate two managers and at least two designers for training during the Pune onsite.
Kill clause - UAT commitment. If the Client's nominated UAT contact is unable or unwilling to commit the minimum 2 hours per week after week 2 of the build, Service Provider reserves the right to pause the engagement and trigger the adoption-risk clause in section 6.2 below. This is a condition of the engagement, not a courtesy request.

  • Hosting and infrastructure pass-through for Service Provider-managed components.

  • AI proposal generator with three-column output (AI draft, historical pattern, final), confidence scoring, deviation flagging beyond 10% from historical patterns, and mandatory human sign-off gate.
  • Rate library with versioning and GST/SAC/HSN tagging.
  • PDF export and WhatsApp-on-approval send flow.
  • Weekly Excel export to Client's Google Drive.
  • Hindi, Marathi and English training videos for the principal user.

Proposal Engine monthly covers AI API usage up to 40 proposal drafts per calendar month, hosting, and L2 support up to 2 hours per calendar month. Target go-live is 4 calendar weeks from kickoff. Client commits a minimum of 1 hour per week of the nominated user's time for UAT during the build.

If Client signs a Proposal Engine SOW and subsequently signs the Studio Command Centre or Command Centre Prepaid SOW within 90 days of Proposal Engine go-live, the full ₹1,50,000 Proposal Engine setup fee shall be credited against the Command Centre setup fee.

4. Change requests

Any addition, modification or deletion to the scope defined in section 1.2 shall be treated as a Change Request ("CR"). The CR process is as follows:

  1. Client submits a written CR (email or documented WhatsApp message) describing the requested change.
  2. Service Provider issues a written impact estimate within 3 business days, detailing scope, schedule impact, and cost.
  3. Client approves the estimate in writing before any work begins.
  4. Change is executed and billed in the next invoice cycle.

Change requests are billed at ₹2,500 per engineering hour, exclusive of GST. No verbal CR shall be honoured under any circumstance.

4.1 Revision limits on deliverables

Each deliverable in section 1.2 is subject to a cap of two (2) rounds of revisions based on Client feedback within the fixed scope. Revisions beyond the second round are treated as Change Requests and billed at ₹2,500 per hour.

4.2 Deemed acceptance

Where Client does not provide written feedback, approval or rejection on a deliverable within 7 business days of sharing, the deliverable shall be deemed accepted.

5. Intellectual property

5.1 Ownership of work product

All source code, configuration, database schema, training videos, runbook PDF and documentation produced specifically for Client under this SOW ("Work Product") shall, upon full payment of all fees due, be the property of the Client, on a work-for-hire basis.

5.2 Retained rights of Service Provider

Service Provider retains the right to use, modify and redistribute:

  • Generic, non-Client-specific components, utilities, libraries, prompt templates and workflow patterns that pre-exist this engagement or are developed by Service Provider for general use.
  • After 90 calendar days of live Client usage, and subject to Client's prior written consent, the right to name iDesign Architects publicly as a case study. Without written consent, Client remains anonymous in all Service Provider marketing.

5.3 Third-party IP

Third-party software, APIs and libraries used in the Platform remain governed by their respective licences. No IP of any third party is transferred to Client under this SOW.

6. Term, termination and adoption

6.1 Term

This SOW commences on the date of signature and remains in force for a minimum period covering the build + 12 months of retainer (Option A) or the 12-month prepaid period (Option B). Thereafter, the arrangement converts to month-to-month on the monthly retainer (Option A) or renews annually (Option B).

6.2 Adoption checkpoint (week 6)

A formal adoption review takes place at the end of week 6 of the build. If fewer than 40% of Client's new projects initiated after build kickoff have been entered into the Command Centre by this date:

  • Service Provider will pause the Team Workflow phase.
  • Service Provider will refund 30% of the Team Workflow phase fees against the then-unearned balance.
  • The parties will in good faith renegotiate to a thinner, AI-proposal-only SKU at a revised fee of ₹1,50,000 (flat), in lieu of the full Command Centre scope.

6.3 Cancellation by Client

Client may terminate this SOW with 30 calendar days written notice. Upon termination:

  • Setup fees already invoiced are non-refundable, except as provided under clause 6.2.
  • Monthly retainer is pro-rated for the termination month.
  • Service Provider shall deliver all Client Work Product (code, credentials, data exports) within 15 business days of the termination date.

6.4 Cancellation by Service Provider

Service Provider may terminate this SOW with 30 calendar days written notice in the event of: material breach by Client; Client insolvency; non-payment beyond 30 days past invoice due date; or Client's failure to fulfil the 2 hours per week UAT commitment (section 2) beyond two consecutive review weeks.

7. Confidentiality

Both parties shall treat as confidential any non-public information shared under this engagement, including client lists, project financials, rate libraries, design work, and the existence and terms of this SOW. A separate Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") may be signed alongside this SOW and shall be read as incorporated herein by reference.

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this SOW for a period of 3 years.

8. Data protection

Service Provider shall handle all Client data (including client contact information, project data, rate libraries and financial records) in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India. Client is the data fiduciary and Service Provider operates as a data processor acting on documented instructions of Client.
